# SelfXpress Documentation

## Introduction
    This project was made for a company focused in technologies, home appliences, gadgets & kids safety, called SelfXpress. 
    They are a mozambican based company that operate in Maputo & Mapauto City and aiming to expand to other regions and break borders, they deliver their products directly to your home or desired location, with safety, care and joy.
    To help expand their business, we have created a website for more online presence in hopes to reach more clients and get more leads with social media integration. 
    At the same time they would like the website to have the look and feel of their brand, through a modern, elegant and minimal, while interactive and responsive design with great user expirience.
    Customers when clicked on a desired item, the user will be redirected to the companies WhatsApp.


## Ojectives 
* Create a mobile responsive website
* Help improve their brand identity 
* Get more leads and conversion 
* More online presence through SEO 
* Improved user experience

## Technologies 
In this first stage of the project we will use a simple stack:

## Wireframe
This website will be composed by 3 pages (Home, About, Products).

### Home
1. Navbar
It has logo on the left, nav-links in the center and a contact us button on the right
2. Hero Carousel Header Section:
Welcome message with a modern look highlighting the company's focus on affordable tech and home appliances, and the benefits of low or no cost delivery.
3. Promotional Banner: 
It's a promotional bar telling the user it is 25% off when buying three items
4. Feauture Section: 
Highlighting the key benefits of shopping with the company such as lower cost and free/low-cost delivery
5. Explore Section: 
Three main ideas:
* Extensive home appliances collection
* Wide Range of Tech Gadgets
* Kid-friendly Gadgets    
6. Benefits Section: 
    Detailed benefits section focusing on cost savings, variety , and delivery perks. 
7. Call To Action (CTA) Section:
Encourage vistors to explore products and make a purchase.
8. Product Carousel Section: 
Product cards inside a carousel 3x3:
*  3 cards shown with the fourth slighty showned;
    *  Gaps between;
    *  No Borders & Border-radius
    * Image top & caption left & down
9. Testimonials Section:
Customer testimonials focusing on satisfaction with products and delivery service.
10. Contact Section: 
The get in touch must have a glassed background and border with an animation when hovered with a light grey border
11. Footer Section: 
Page list in the left next to it socials , next to it locations, logo with a big text bellow.
